Order decomposition of the Shapley values from fully enumerated oracles
error normalised by max_i |phi_i|

phi_i = sum_{A containing i} m(A)/|A|, split by |A| -- exact, no sampling
trunc(q) = max_i |phi_i - phi_i^{<=q}| / max_i |phi_i|
  = the error a PERFECT q-th-order model would still carry
